# Motor-Vehicle-Collisions-Analysis-Dashboard
This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of motor vehicle crash trends and their year-over-year performance. It highlights key safety indicators such as total crashes, injuries, and fatalities, enabling quick assessment of improvements or deteriorations in road safety across time.

The upper KPI section displays real-time performance metrics, including Total Crashes (Year-to-Date) and their percentage change compared to the same period last year. Green arrows indicate a reduction in incidents (improvement), while red arrows flag an increase (risk escalation).
The trend visuals illustrate monthly crash patterns, allowing comparison between current and previous year trends using time-intelligence DAX functions (SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, TOTALYTD). Users can identify seasonal spikes, recurring causes, and boroughs with higher crash concentrations.

This data-driven view empowers analysts and city planners to:
Detect emerging safety concerns
Track progress of safety initiatives
Prioritize interventions in high-risk areas
Communicate results through clear, color-coded KPIs
